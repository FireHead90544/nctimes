A single April week brought six unrelated founders to Nikhil Chandra's inbox, each asking some version of the same question: how do we grow without breaking what already works?

Rather than take six identical one-off calls, he restructured the offering into a standing monthly cohort — same diagnostic, shared format, individual follow-through. Five of the six founders are still in it.

The cohort meets monthly. Each session uses the same diagnostic framework applied to one founder's constraint, with input from the others. The cross-pollination is, in his account, the part that actually works: founders who are not competing with each other can be honest in ways that individual advisory calls rarely produce.

The offering has since expanded. It is not cheap and not available to everyone who asks. The qualification criteria are simple: the founder has to be working on a real constraint, not looking for validation of a decision already made.

<InfoBox title="Advisory Cohort Format">
- **Frequency** — Monthly sessions
- **Size** — 6–8 founders per cohort
- **Format** — One constraint per session, group diagnostic
- **Duration** — Year-long commitment
- **Qualification** — Real constraint required, not validation
</InfoBox>
