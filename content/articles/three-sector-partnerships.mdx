Healthcare, logistics and D2C retail — three sectors Nikhil Chandra had deliberately avoided until this quarter, when all three arrived within the same fortnight.

The avoidance was not arbitrary. Each sector has structural features that make growth consulting harder: healthcare's regulatory complexity, logistics' asset-intensity, D2C's dependence on marketing spend over operations. He had been waiting until he had enough adjacent experience to work in all three without the sector being the thing that makes the engagement difficult.

Each new partnership begins, as ever, with the diagnostic before the deck. Early-stage scoping for all three is underway for Q3 2026. None of the three engagements will be described publicly in detail until they are complete and the client has reviewed what will be said.

The expansion into these sectors reflects a deliberate choice: to be useful in more of the places where growth constraints actually exist, not just the ones where consulting is already comfortable.
