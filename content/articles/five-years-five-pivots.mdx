A short account of the five decisions that, in hindsight, mattered more than they felt like at the time.

None of the five looked like a milestone while it was happening. Most looked like minor scheduling decisions that, a year later, turned out to have quietly redirected everything after them. This is, in his experience, how careers actually work — not a sequence of clear choices but a series of small bets, some of which compound.

**2022** — The first independent mandate. Taken on, he will admit, before he was entirely ready, which turned out to be exactly the right time to take it on. The readiness came from doing it, not from waiting until he was sure.

**2023** — A deliberate shift from operations-first consulting to growth strategy work. This required saying no to a category of work he was competent at in order to build competence in the one he wanted. The gap year, financially, was uncomfortable. Worth it.

**2024** — The first mentorship cohort. Twenty seats filled in three days. He learned more from running it than he expected, mostly because the people he was teaching were asking questions he had stopped asking.

**2025–2026** — Forty completed engagements. TheNCTimes. A wider range of sectors. The diagnostic, unchanged in its steps, now faster to execute because the pattern recognition has deepened.

The fifth pivot is still in progress. He is not ready to write about it yet.
