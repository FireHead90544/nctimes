Nikhil Chandra joined three other panelists to argue that "future of work" conversations usually skip the only question that matters: future of work for whom?

His remarks focused deliberately on mid-cap firms, a segment he felt was missing entirely from the panel's premise. Most future-of-work discussion, he observed, is written by people at large enterprises for other people at large enterprises. The constraints and the opportunities are entirely different at 500 employees versus 50,000.

The panel covered remote work, AI adoption, and generational shift in workplace expectation. On each topic, he tried to reframe the question from the mid-cap perspective: not what is technically possible, but what is economically viable and operationally realistic for a firm that does not have a dedicated HR technology function.
