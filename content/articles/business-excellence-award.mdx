The honour cites "sustained, measurable impact across sectors not known for sharing a consultant" — language the awarding committee apparently did not intend as a joke, though Nikhil Chandra read it as one.

He was nominated, somewhat to his surprise, by two former clients independently. His acceptance remarks ran under ninety seconds and mentioned his mentees more than they mentioned him.

The award is given annually by a body of industry practitioners and independent reviewers. The nomination process is blind to self-applications, which is why Nikhil Chandra heard about his own nomination from the committee rather than from his own calendar.

He keeps the award in his office, on a shelf that also contains a broken whiteboard marker from the first engagement that worked, and a client's handwritten note from the one that didn't. He considers all three equally instructive.
