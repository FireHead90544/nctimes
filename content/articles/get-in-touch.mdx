TheNCTimes takes letters. New consulting enquiries, speaking invitations and the occasional correction are all welcome — the fastest route is email, listed in the classifieds column on Page 4.

Mentorship cohort applications are handled separately and open each January; speaking invitations should include expected audience size and format up front. This is not a filter — it is because the format of the event changes what is worth saying, and preparing the right talk takes longer than a week.

New consulting enquiries are reviewed within five business days. The response will be a short note with one of three outcomes: an initial conversation scheduled, a direct referral to someone better suited to the problem, or a note that the current workload doesn't allow for a new engagement. All three are honest answers. The third is not a door closing permanently.
